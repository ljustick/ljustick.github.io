**NFixPlanet** is an open database and bioinformatics toolkit for investigating the diversity, distribution, and ecology of nitrogen-fixing microorganisms (_diazotrophs_) across global ecosystems.

I led the development of NFixPlanet, including the construction of a global diazotroph genome catalogue and the development of computational pipelines for identifying, annotating, and mapping diazotrophs in genomic and metagenomic datasets.

The project provides an integrated framework connecting a curated reference database, open-source software, and a web resource for exploring nitrogen-fixing microorganisms across environments.
