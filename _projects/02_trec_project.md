## Traversing European Coastlines (TREC)

**TREC (Traversing European Coastlines)** is an EMBL-led expedition investigating the biodiversity and molecular responses of organisms and microbial communities across European coastal ecosystems.

The expedition brings together researchers and institutions across Europe to sample coastal environments at an unprecedented geographic scale, integrating molecular, ecological, and environmental measurements to better understand how changing environments shape biological communities.

### My role

I was a member of the **TREC field operations team**, contributing directly to the expedition and large-scale collection of environmental samples across European coastlines.

Building on the resulting TREC dataset, I am now a member of the TREC consortium, where I lead a research project investigating the diversity, distribution, and environmental drivers of nitrogen-fixing microorganisms (diazotrophs) across European coastal ecosystems.
