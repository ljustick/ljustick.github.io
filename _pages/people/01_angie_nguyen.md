## Angie Nguyen

**Undergraduate Researcher · 2020–2022**

Angie conducted undergraduate research through the UCI Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP), investigating the global phylogeography and microdiversity of marine nitrogen-fixing photoautotrophs. During her research, she received a UROP research scholarship, presented her work at the ASLO Ocean Sciences Meeting, and went on to publish the project as a first-author peer-reviewed manuscript.

**Research project:** Nguyen A, Ustick L, Larkin A, Martiny A. (2025). [Global phylogeography and microdiversity of the marine diazotrophic photoautotrophs Trichodesmium and UCYN-A](https://journals.asm.org/doi/full/10.1128/msphere.00245-25). _mSphere_. [doi:10.1128/msphere.00245-25](https://doi.org/10.1128/msphere.00245-25)

**Current position:** MD/PhD Student, University of California Irvine
